On the iPad

  
iPadOS has Apple MIDI built in, but an app has to turn its network session on. AUM does not; a free app such as midimittr does (Network, Session: on). The Push then shows up in that app's directory under its name: tap it to connect. In AUM pick Network Session 1 as a MIDI input or output.

  
For the Push to connect by itself (Connect above), the iPad must allow connections from anyone (midimittr: "Who may connect: Anyone").

  
Messages from the iPad carry the time they were sent; the Push plays them at that time plus the buffer. 10 ms suits a good Wi-Fi; raise it if notes stutter or the page counts many late packets, lower it for a wired link.

  
Over Wi-Fi expect a few milliseconds of jitter. Use Ableton Link for tempo, not MIDI clock. Live sends clock to Net MIDI only with Sync on for it in Settings, MIDI.
